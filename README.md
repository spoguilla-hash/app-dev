# My Favorite Movie: *Mean Girls*

**Mean Girls** is a teen comedy film written by Tina Fey, based on the non-fiction book *Queen Bees and Wannabes* by Rosalind Wiseman. It explores high school social dynamics and the impact of cliques on young women.

## Plot Summary
- **Cady Heron** (played by Lindsay Lohan), a teenager who was homeschooled in Africa, moves to the United States and enters a high school for the first time.
- She befriends **Janice Ian** and **Damian Leigh** but later gets entangled with the school's most popular girls, the "Plastics," led by the ruthless **Regina George**.

## Main Characters
1. **Cady Heron** - The main protagonist who navigates the ups and downs of high school life.
2. **Regina George** - The leader of the "Plastics," known for her manipulative ways.
3. **Janice Ian** - Cady's quirky, rebellious friend who doesn't fit in with the "Plastics."
4. **Damian Leigh** - Janice's best friend and a comedic character who helps Cady navigate the social scene.

## Why I Love It
*Mean Girls* is a hilarious and relatable film that cleverly tackles the social challenges of high school. The witty humor, unforgettable quotes, and sharp commentary on teenage behavior make it a standout comedy.

### Famous Line
> "On Wednesdays, we wear pink." – The Plastics
